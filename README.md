# Space Odyssey · The Last Guardian

## Story Plot

A mysterious signal turns the planetary guardians against the worlds they were built to protect. With Earth next in line, Commander Reyes sends the Guardian to Mars. The pilot fights through the solar system, navigates the asteroid belt, and follows the signal back to its source, the Architect. The Guardian returns to defend Earth in a two-phase battle.

After the battle, Mira recovers a distress call from the **Ibis**, a survey vessel that vanished near the Kuiper Belt nineteen years ago. Reyes recognizes a signal from her missing sister. She joins the Guardian, Mira, and Sol aboard the **Expedition Shuttle**. The crew passes Saturn and Neptune, crosses a wormhole beyond the Kuiper Belt, and docks at a relay under the twin suns of Alpha Centauri.

## Characters and Ships

| Character | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Guardian** | The player character, Earth's last available pilot and the pilot of the rescue mission. |
| **Commander Reyes** | Leads Earth's defense, briefs the Guardian, and joins the rescue mission to find her sister. |
| **Mira** | The crew's engineer. She recovers the distress recording and keeps the relay operational. |
| **Sol** | The navigator who plots the route to Alpha Centauri. |
| **Ship AI** | The onboard assistant that guides the crew, identifies the Ibis signal, and confirms docking. |
| **The Architect** | The creator of the planetary guardians and the final boss at Earth. |

The corrupted planetary bosses are **Crimson Warden** (Mars), **Storm Warden** (Jupiter), **Ringkeeper** (Saturn), **Frost Sentinel** (Uranus), **Tide Warden** (Neptune), **Sulfur Warden** (Venus), and **Solar Warden** (Mercury).

| Spaceship | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Expedition Shuttle** | The player's ship for asteroid flight and the rescue mission to Alpha Centauri. |
| **Ibis** | The missing survey vessel whose distress call launches the rescue mission. |

## How to Run

**Play offline.** Extract the ZIP and open `Play_Space_Odyssey.html` in your browser. No installation is required.

**Run from source.** With Node.js 22 or 24 installed, open a terminal in the folder containing `package.json` and run:

```bash
npm ci
npm run dev
```
